ADMINISTRATIVE NOTES — REMOVE THIS BLOCK BEFORE PRINTING FOR SIGNATURE
行政注意事項——列印供簽署前請刪除本段
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Drafted in-house; NOT reviewed by a lawyer qualified in the Republic of
   China (Taiwan). Have it reviewed before relying on it. It engages the right
   of personality and likeness under Article 18 of the Civil Code and the
   Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), whose Article 8 requires that we tell
   the person, at the point of collection, who we are and what we will do with
   their data. Part D of this form is that notice.
2. Consent here is genuinely optional. Declining it, or withdrawing it later,
   must have no effect whatsoever on a person's membership, place in a class,
   strip time or treatment. If a coach or administrator ever implies
   otherwise, that is a serious matter, not a misunderstanding.
3. Do not bundle this with the liability waiver. Separate forms, separate
   signatures, separate decisions.
4. Honor the granular boxes in Part B literally. "No video" means no video,
   including a clip where the person is in the background of somebody else's
   bout. If a session is being filmed, say so at the start of the session and
   offer a place on the floor that is out of frame.
5. NO RECORDING IN CHANGING AREAS, EVER. This is a standing club rule, not a
   consent question, and it binds coaches, members, parents and visitors alike.
   No consent box on this form authorizes it and none can. It is the one media
   rule that must be enforced without asking whether anybody minded.
6. Withdrawal is prospective. We must take material down from our own channels
   promptly, and we must tell the person honestly what we cannot claw back —
   reshares, third-party reposts, printed matter already distributed, and
   search-engine or archive caches.
7. Keep the signed original for as long as we use the material plus five
   years. For a person under 18, keep it until they turn 18 plus the limitation
   period: a consent signed for an eight-year-old outlives a five-year file.
   Maintain a media consent register keyed to membership records so that anyone
   editing a post can check status in seconds; this is the only way rule 4
   survives contact with a Friday deadline.
8. The club publishes bouts, lessons and results as a matter of principle
   (docs/mission_statement.txt, Pillar 01). That principle does not override
   anyone's decision on this form. Open means open about the club, not open
   with other people's faces.
