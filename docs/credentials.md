# Credentials, insurance and renewals register

Internal. Started 16 September 2026.

What the club holds, who holds it, and when it expires. It exists because three
other documents make promises that depend on it: the waiver's administrative
note 7 (we must be able to *show* we take the precautions we claim), Part B.7 of
the minor safeguarding consent (police record certificates for everyone working
with children), and components 2.4 and 3.2 of the mission statement, where coach
credentials and equipment inspection are still tagged `[NEED]`.

An expiry date that lives only in someone's memory is not evidence. Fill the
blanks in as the documents come in, and scan each one into the club records.

## How this is maintained

- Reviewed at the same time as the mission statement, every six months, and
  whenever a certificate is issued or renewed.
- Flag anything inside 90 days of expiry at the review; do not wait for it to
  lapse.
- A lapsed first-aid or CPR certificate does not stop a session, but it must be
  recorded here as lapsed on the day it lapses, and renewed before the next
  review. Silence in this file is treated as "not held", not as "fine".
- A lapsed 警察刑事紀錄證明書 does stop that person working with children under
  Part B.7 of the safeguarding form, because that is what the form promises
  guardians.
- When a row here flips to held, update the matching evidence tag in
  `mission_statement.txt` and `mission_statement.zh-TW.txt` in the same commit.
