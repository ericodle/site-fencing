ADMINISTRATIVE NOTES — REMOVE THIS BLOCK BEFORE PRINTING FOR SIGNATURE
行政注意事項——列印供簽署前請刪除本段
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. This form was drafted in-house and has NOT been reviewed by a lawyer
   qualified in the Republic of China (Taiwan). Have it reviewed before it is
   relied on. Under Article 222 of the Civil Code, liability for intentional
   acts or gross negligence cannot be waived in advance, and Article 247-1 and
   the Consumer Protection Act limit standard terms that are unfairly
   disadvantageous to the signer. This form is written to stay inside those
   limits: it does not purport to excuse our own gross negligence, and it must
   not be redrafted to try to.

2. WHAT CLAUSE 10 IS ACTUALLY WORTH. Do not let anyone at the club treat a
   signed waiver as a shield. Under Article 7 of the Consumer Protection Act a
   service provider owes the safety that current professional standards
   reasonably support; liability to an injured consumer is joint and several,
   and our own fault reduces damages rather than removing them. The
   regulator-issued standard contract for fitness centers lists as a PROHIBITED
   clause any term excluding or limiting the operator's liability for injury to
   a consumer's body, health or property caused by its services or equipment,
   and Taiwanese courts have held operators liable despite a signed waiver
   where an obvious safety measure was missing. Against a member hurt by our
   own shortfall, Clause 10 is close to inert. Its real value is evidentiary:
   it proves the member was told what the risks were. Fix the equipment.

3. REVIEW PERIOD. Article 11-1 of the Consumer Protection Act requires a
   reasonable period, WITHIN thirty days, for the signer to read a standard-form
   contract — thirty days is the ceiling, not the target, and a clause making
   the signer waive the period is void. The nearest sector benchmark, the
   fitness-center standard contract, sets at least three days. Give at least
   three days, longer for anything tied to a paid course, never present this
   form for the first time at the door of a session the person has already paid
   for and traveled to, and always fill in the hand-over date in Part C — that
   field is the only evidence that a period was given.

4. COMPLETE THE INSURANCE DISCLOSURE IN CLAUSE 7 BEFORE PRINTING. If the club
   carries no policy, the form must say so in those words. Leaving it blank, or
   printing a form that only disclaims cover, is not acceptable: the Sports
   Administration's standard for organized events is that the organizer
   discloses the scope of its public liability insurance and tells participants
   to arrange their own.

5. RETENTION. Keep the signed original for at least five years for an adult
   participant. For a participant under 18, keep it until they turn 18 plus the
   limitation period — a minor's claim runs from majority, so a file closed at
   five years can expire before the claim it exists to answer. Store scans with
   the membership record, access restricted to club administration.

6. SEPARATE FORMS. A separate signature is required on the media release
   (docs/media_release.txt), and a third form — the minor safeguarding consent
   (docs/minor_safeguarding_consent.txt) — is required for every participant
   under 18. Do not merge them: physical risk, being photographed, and how the
   club may be alone with a child are different decisions, and bundling them
   makes all three easier to challenge.

7. Retain the corresponding evidence that we take the stated precautions —
   equipment inventory and inspection dates, coach qualifications, incident log
   (see docs/mission_statement.txt, components 2.4 and 3.2). A waiver signed by
   a club that cannot show it maintained its masks is worth very little.

1. THE ACTIVITY 活動內容

EN  Fencing is a combat sport. Participants attack each other with steel
    blades, at speed, on a narrow strip, while wearing protective equipment.
    Training includes footwork, conditioning, drills, free bouting against
    other fencers of varying size, strength and experience, and competition.
    Sessions take place indoors at partner venues, outdoors in public parks,
    and at pop-up locations around Taipei, on floors that are not always
    purpose-built for sport.

ZH  擊劍是一項對抗性運動。參加者身著護具，在狹窄的劍道上以鋼製劍身高速
    互相攻擊。訓練內容包括步法、體能、分解練習、與體型、力量與經驗各異的
    劍手自由對打，以及比賽。課程進行地點包括合作場地的室內空間、公共公園的
    戶外場地，以及台北各地的快閃場地，其地面未必為運動用途所設計。

2. RISKS I ACCEPT 本人承擔之風險

EN  I understand that fencing carries risks that cannot be eliminated, however
    carefully the club runs a session. These include, without limitation:

    eye injury; puncture or laceration wounds, including from a blade that
    breaks during a bout and from a blade that penetrates or passes around
    protective clothing; collisions with other fencers, walls, equipment or
    the ground; cuts from broken blades handled off the strip; electric shock
    or burns from scoring equipment and cabling; heat exhaustion, heatstroke,
    dehydration, sunburn, insect bites and slips on wet ground at outdoor
    sessions; aggravation of a pre-existing condition; cardiac events;
    permanent disability; and death. I accept that these risks arise from my
    own conduct, from the conduct of other participants, from equipment
    failure, from the condition of the venue and from ordinary accident.

ZH  本人了解，無論俱樂部如何謹慎安排課程，擊劍仍存在無法完全排除之風險，

    腦震盪與頭部撞擊；牙齒與顏面傷害；眼部傷害；穿刺或撕裂傷，包括對打中

    來自本人自身行為、其他參加者之行為、器材故障、場地狀況或一般意外。

4. RULES, EQUIPMENT AND CONDUCT 規則、裝備與行為

    blade or spar outside a coached session or without a mask; never point a
    blade at a person who is not masked; carry a blade with the point down and
    never run with one; never put a mask on while holding a blade; fence at a

    may lend me equipment, that I must return it and report any damage, and

    competition. I will not return to fencing, including footwork drills
