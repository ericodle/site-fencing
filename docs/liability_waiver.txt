ADMINISTRATIVE NOTES — REMOVE THIS BLOCK BEFORE PRINTING FOR SIGNATURE
行政注意事項——列印供簽署前請刪除本段
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. This form was drafted in-house and has NOT been reviewed by a lawyer
   qualified in the Republic of China (Taiwan). Have it reviewed before it is
   relied on. Under Article 222 of the Civil Code, liability for intentional
   acts or gross negligence cannot be waived in advance, and Article 247-1 and
   the Consumer Protection Act limit standard terms that are unfairly
   disadvantageous to the signer. This form is written to stay inside those
   limits: it does not purport to excuse our own gross negligence, and it must
   not be redrafted to try to.
2. Because it is a standard-form contract offered to consumers, give the signer
   a reasonable period to review it before signing — 30 days is the safe
   benchmark — and never present it for the first time at the door of a session
   the person has already paid for and traveled to.
3. Keep the signed original for at least five years. Store scans with the
   membership record, access restricted to club administration.
4. A separate signature is required on the media release
   (docs/media_release.txt). Do not merge the two forms: consent to be
   photographed and acceptance of physical risk are different decisions, and
   bundling them makes both easier to challenge.
5. Retain the corresponding evidence that we take the stated precautions —
   equipment inventory and inspection dates, coach qualifications, incident log
   (see docs/mission_statement.txt, components 2.4 and 3.2). A waiver signed by
   a club that cannot show it maintained its masks is worth very little.

1. THE ACTIVITY 活動內容

EN  Fencing is a combat sport. Participants attack each other with steel
    blades, at speed, on a narrow strip, while wearing protective equipment.
    Training includes footwork, conditioning, drills, free bouting against
    other fencers of varying size, strength and experience, and competition.
    Sessions take place indoors at partner venues, outdoors in public parks,
    and at pop-up locations around Taipei, on floors that are not always
    purpose-built for sport.

ZH  擊劍是一項對抗性運動。參加者身著護具，在狹窄的劍道上以鋼製劍身高速
    互相攻擊。訓練內容包括步法、體能、分解練習、與體型、力量與經驗各異的
    劍手自由對打，以及比賽。課程進行地點包括合作場地的室內空間、公共公園的
    戶外場地，以及台北各地的快閃場地，其地面未必為運動用途所設計。

2. RISKS I ACCEPT 本人承擔之風險

EN  I understand that fencing carries risks that cannot be eliminated, however
    carefully the club runs a session. These include, without limitation:
    bruising, welts and abrasions; sprains, strains and joint injuries; muscle
    tears; fractures; concussion and head impact; dental and facial injury;
    eye injury; puncture or laceration wounds, including from a blade that
    breaks during a bout and from a blade that penetrates or passes around
    protective clothing; collisions with other fencers, walls, equipment or
    the ground; cuts from broken blades handled off the strip; electric shock
    or burns from scoring equipment and cabling; heat exhaustion, heatstroke,
    dehydration, sunburn, insect bites and slips on wet ground at outdoor
    sessions; aggravation of a pre-existing condition; cardiac events;
    permanent disability; and death. I accept that these risks arise from my
    own conduct, from the conduct of other participants, from equipment
    failure, from the condition of the venue and from ordinary accident.

ZH  本人了解，無論俱樂部如何謹慎安排課程，擊劍仍存在無法完全排除之風險，
    包括但不限於：瘀傷、鞭痕與擦傷；扭傷、拉傷與關節傷害；肌肉撕裂；骨折；
    腦震盪與頭部撞擊；牙齒與顏面傷害；眼部傷害；穿刺或撕裂傷，包括對打中
    劍身斷裂所致，以及劍身穿透護具或自護具邊緣繞入所致；與其他劍手、牆面、
    器材或地面之碰撞；於劍道外處理斷劍所致之割傷；因計分器材與線材所致之
    電擊或灼傷；戶外課程之熱衰竭、中暑、脫水、曬傷、蟲咬與濕滑地面跌倒；
    既有傷病之惡化；心血管急症；永久失能；以及死亡。本人接受上述風險可能
    來自本人自身行為、其他參加者之行為、器材故障、場地狀況或一般意外。

4. RULES, EQUIPMENT AND CONDUCT 規則、裝備與行為

EN  I agree to: follow the instructions of coaches and session leaders at all
    times; fence only with protective equipment that meets the standard the
    club requires for that activity, correctly worn and fully fastened,
    including mask, jacket, glove, plastron where required, and chest
    protection where required; inspect my own equipment before use and report
    any damage; stop immediately when "halt" is called; never fence, handle a
    blade or spar outside a coached session or without a mask; never take part
    while under the influence of alcohol or drugs; treat every other
    participant, including guests from other clubs, with respect. I understand
    that the club may lend me equipment, that I must return it and report any
    damage, and that borrowed equipment does not transfer responsibility for
    my safe conduct to the club.

ZH  本人同意：全程遵守教練與課堂負責人之指示；僅於穿戴符合本會就該活動所
    要求標準之護具、且正確穿著並完整扣合之情況下擊劍，包括面罩、劍衣、
    手套、必要時之護腋（plastron）與護胸；使用前自行檢查裝備並回報任何
    毀損；聽到「停（halt）」時立即停止；不得於無教練指導之情況下、或未戴
    面罩時擊劍、持劍或對練；不得於受酒精或藥物影響時參與活動；尊重所有其他
    參加者，包括其他俱樂部之來訪劍手。本人了解俱樂部可能出借裝備，本人應
    歸還並回報毀損，且借用裝備並不因此將本人安全行為之責任轉由俱樂部承擔。

    費用。本人確認已自行投保醫療與意外保險，或自願承擔未投保之全部後果。
