# Session 5 — Bout-length repeat effort

60 minutes · conditioning bias · moderate nervous-system cost · needs a flat
strip, a bench, a jump rope, two cones

A direct-elimination bout is three periods of three minutes with a minute
between them, and inside each period the work comes in bursts of two to eight
seconds separated by a few seconds of nothing. This session trains that shape:
repeat efforts, short recoveries, and the ability to make the last minute of the
third period look like the first minute of the first.

It is the only session in the set where being tired is the point. It is still
not a session to grind — the intervals are timed so that quality holds.

**Marker for the log:** burst count in the final round of block B against the
first round. Anything inside 10% is a good day.

**Stop rule:** technique, not fatigue. When the en garde collapses — back knee
falling in, torso leaning — walk that interval and rejoin the next one.

## 0:00–0:06 · Raise

Six minutes of jogging that gets progressively quicker: two minutes easy, two
minutes steady, one minute at a pace where talking gets awkward, one minute easy
again. Breathe through the nose for as long as it stays comfortable.

## 0:06–0:11 · Mobility, one minute each

1. Inchworm walkouts, 8, with a push-up at the bottom of the last three
2. World's greatest stretch, 30 s each side
3. Leg swings, 15 each direction each leg
4. Deep squat to stand, 10, reaching overhead at the top
5. Thoracic rotations over the bench, 10 each side

## 0:11–0:15 · Activation, two rounds of two minutes

- 20 pogo hops
- 10 bodyweight squats, slow down and fast up
- 8 push-ups
- 20 s hang from the bar
- Rest the remainder of the two minutes

## 0:15–0:27 · Block A — Three periods

Three three-minute periods of continuous footwork with a minute of rest between
each, then a minute of rest before block B. This is the closest thing in the
week to an actual bout.

Inside each period, keep the en garde the whole time and work in bursts: a few
seconds of preparation, one committed action, then a reset. Do not jog the three
minutes — it is footwork throughout, self-called, with real intent.

- **Period 1 — preparation.** Advances, retreats, changes of rhythm. One lunge
  roughly every 20 seconds.
- **Period 2 — attacking.** Every 15 seconds, a committed attack: advance-lunge,
  balestra-lunge, or double advance into a lunge.
- **Period 3 — defending and answering.** Retreat under pressure, then answer
  with a retreat-lunge or a counter-attack every 15 seconds.

Count your committed actions in each period. Periods 2 and 3 should match.

## 0:27–0:39 · Block B — Touch intervals

Twelve rounds on the minute: 15 seconds all-out, 45 seconds walking. This is the
work-to-rest ratio of a real bout, held for twelve exchanges.

Set a cone at each end of a 10 m run. Each 15-second burst: from en garde at one
cone, advance-lunge, recover, sprint to the far cone, turn, sprint back, en
garde, lunge again. Count the lunges completed in the 15 seconds.

Record round 1 and round 12. That pair of numbers is the session marker and, run
week after week, is the clearest evidence of conditioning the club can collect
without any equipment at all.

## 0:39–0:51 · Block C — Mixed circuit

Four rounds of three minutes. Work straight through, then rest what is left of
the round — usually 45 to 75 seconds. If a round leaves no rest at all, cut the
reps by a third rather than running into the next round.

- 10 squat jumps
- 10 push-ups
- 20 mountain climbers
- 10 m bear crawl out, 10 m crab walk back
- 30 s jump rope, fast
- 10 bench step-ups each leg

## 0:51–0:56 · Block D — Last point

Five rounds: 20 seconds of maximum-intent footwork, 40 seconds of rest. Pick one
attacking phrase before each round and run it repeatedly for the 20 seconds, as
if the score were tied in the last minute.

Quality falls off here and that is the training effect. Keep the point on line
and the recovery balanced even when the legs are asking not to.

## 0:56–1:00 · Cool-down

Two minutes of walking until the breathing is normal again, then quad stretch
30 s each side, hamstring on the bench 30 s each side, hip flexor 30 s each side.
Finish lying with calves on the bench for a minute of slow breathing.
