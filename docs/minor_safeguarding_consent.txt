ADMINISTRATIVE NOTES — REMOVE THIS BLOCK BEFORE PRINTING FOR SIGNATURE
行政注意事項——列印供簽署前請刪除本段
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Part B is POLICY, not consent. It binds the club whether or not a guardian
   signs anything, and it is not negotiable down for a particular family or a
   particular coach. Part C is the consent: the specific activities a guardian
   authorizes for their own child.
2. This form exists because the club sells one-on-one private lessons to
   children, organizes carpools and shared rooms on Discord for away events,
   and runs sessions in public parks. Those are the three situations in which
   an adult is alone with, or effectively in charge of, somebody else's child.
   The model is USA Fencing's General Consent Form under the U.S. Center for
   SafeSport's Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policies, which requires written
   parental consent for exactly these activities and constrains how they are
   conducted. Taiwan has no direct equivalent requirement for a private club;
   we adopt the standard anyway.
3. The rules in Part B are only real if the club can show it followed them.
   Keep the session log in B.2, and record the exceptions — an unplanned
   one-to-one, a last-minute lift home — at the time, not afterwards.
4. Do not merge this with the liability waiver or the media release.
5. Coach vetting (B.7) is a commitment the club is making. Do not print this
   form until the club actually holds the documents it promises to hold.
6. Retention: keep until the participant turns 18 plus the limitation period.
7. Not reviewed by a lawyer qualified in Taiwan. Have it reviewed together with
   the other two forms, and have the reviewer check it against the Protection
   of Children and Youths Welfare and Rights Act (兒童及少年福利與權益保障法)
   and its reporting duties.

PART B — WHAT THE CLUB UNDERTAKES 俱樂部之承諾（政策，非同意事項）
================================================================================
These rules apply to every child at the club. They are not waived by signing
this form, and no coach may set them aside.
下列規則適用於本會所有兒童，不因簽署本同意書而免除，任何教練均不得逕予排除。

B.1 OBSERVABLE AND INTERRUPTIBLE 可被看見、可被打斷

EN  Any training session between an adult and a child takes place where it can
    be observed and interrupted by another adult. Private lessons happen on an
    open floor with others present, or in a room with the door open and a
    second adult on the premises who knows the lesson is happening. A coach is
    never alone with a child behind a closed door.

ZH  成人與兒童之間的任何訓練，均須在其他成人得以看見並得以隨時進入打斷的
    環境中進行。個人課應於有他人在場之開放場地進行，或於門保持開啟、且場內
    另有知悉該堂課正在進行之成人時進行。教練不得於門戶關閉之空間單獨與兒童
    相處。

B.2 EXCEPTIONS ARE LOGGED 例外情形須紀錄

EN  If a one-to-one situation happens that was not planned — a parent is late,
    a session empties out, a child needs a lift — the coach moves to a public
    space, contacts the guardian as soon as practicable, and records it in the
    club log the same day: who, when, where, how long, and why.

ZH  如發生非預期之一對一情形——家長遲到、課堂人員散去、兒童需要搭載——
    教練應移至公共空間、儘速聯繫法定代理人，並於當日記錄於俱樂部紀錄簿：
    何人、何時、何地、歷時多久、原因為何。

B.3 ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION 電子通訊

EN  Club business with a child goes to a group channel, or to the guardian, or
    to a channel that includes the guardian. Coaches and club adults do not
    exchange private one-to-one messages with a child on LINE, Instagram,
    Discord, WhatsApp or anywhere else, and do not communicate with a child on
    a platform the guardian has not approved in Part D. Messages are sent at
    reasonable hours. Nothing is deleted to hide it.

ZH  俱樂部與兒童之聯繫，應透過群組頻道、逕向法定代理人，或使用包含法定代理人
    之頻道進行。教練與俱樂部成人不得以 LINE、Instagram、Discord、WhatsApp 或
    其他任何管道與兒童進行一對一私訊，亦不得使用法定代理人未於 D 部分同意之
    平台與兒童聯繫。訊息應於合理時間發送，且不得為掩飾而刪除訊息紀錄。

B.4 CHANGING AREAS AND RECORDING 更衣區域與錄影

EN  No adult is alone with a child in a changing area. No camera or recording
    function is used in a changing area, toilet, shower or any space being used
    to change clothes, by anyone, with or without consent. Children change in a
    designated area, and a coach who needs to speak to a child takes it
    outside that area.

ZH  成人不得於更衣區域內與兒童單獨相處。任何人於更衣區域、廁所、淋浴間，
    或任何正供更衣使用之空間，均不得使用拍照或錄影功能，不論是否取得同意。
    兒童應於指定區域更衣；教練如需與兒童談話，應於該區域外為之。

B.5 PHYSICAL CONTACT IN COACHING 教學中之肢體接觸

EN  Fencing coaching involves touch: correcting a guard, adjusting a grip,
    fitting a mask or jacket, checking a bruise, applying first aid. Contact is
    limited to what the coaching or the injury requires, is done in the open,
    and is explained to the child first. A child may say no to being touched
    and the coach will find another way to teach the point. The club does not
    provide massage, rubdowns or physical therapy of any kind.

ZH  擊劍教學涉及肢體接觸：修正實戰姿勢、調整握把、協助穿戴面罩或劍衣、
    檢視瘀傷、施行急救等。接觸應以教學或傷處所必要者為限，於公開環境進行，
    並事先向兒童說明。兒童得拒絕被碰觸，教練應改以其他方式教授該項技術。
    本會不提供按摩、推拿或任何形式之物理治療。

B.6 TRAVEL AND OVERNIGHT STAYS 交通與過夜

EN  A child is not transported alone in a vehicle with one unrelated adult. At
    away events, a child shares a room only with other children of similar age
    and the same gender identity as the child and guardian have stated, never
    with an adult who is not their own family. Room checks are done by two
    adults, at the door.

ZH  兒童不得與一名非親屬之成人單獨同車。於外地賽事，兒童僅得與年齡相近、
    且性別認同與兒童及法定代理人所述相符之其他兒童同房，不得與其本人家屬
    以外之成人同房。查房應由兩名成人共同於門口進行。

B.7 WHO WORKS WITH CHILDREN 與兒童工作之人員

EN  Every coach and every adult volunteer who works with children provides the
    club with a police criminal record certificate (警察刑事紀錄證明書) before
    starting and every three years after, and the club keeps it on file. The
    club does not place an adult with children while a safeguarding concern
    about them is unresolved.

ZH  所有與兒童工作之教練與成人志工，均須於任職前及其後每三年，向俱樂部
    提出警察刑事紀錄證明書，並由俱樂部存檔。凡涉及兒少保護疑慮尚未釐清者，
    俱樂部不得安排其與兒童接觸。

B.8 REPORTING 通報與申訴

EN  A child, a guardian or any member may raise a concern with the designated
    safeguarding contact below, who is not the coach on the floor. Concerns
    raised in good faith carry no penalty of any kind. Where there is reason to
    suspect a child has been harmed, the club reports to the authorities under
    the Protection of Children and Youths Welfare and Rights Act, and does so
    whether or not the guardian asks it to and whether or not the child is one
    of ours.

    Designated safeguarding contact 兒少保護指定窗口:
      Name 姓名: ___________________________________________________________
      Contact 聯絡方式: ____________________________________________________
      Alternate (not the same person) 備位窗口（不得為同一人）:
      ______________________________________________________________________
    National reporting line 全國保護專線: 113

ZH  兒童、法定代理人或任何會員，均得向下列兒少保護指定窗口反映；該窗口不得
    為當時在場授課之教練。基於善意提出之反映，不生任何不利後果。凡有事實
    足認兒童遭受傷害者，俱樂部應依兒童及少年福利與權益保障法通報主管機關；
    不論法定代理人是否要求，亦不論該名兒童是否為本會學員，均應通報。
